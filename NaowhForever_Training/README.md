# Training Planner

Every spell your class still has to learn: what you can train now, what each level brings and what
it costs, with the trainer's own prices once you have visited one. A road to 60 with a dot for every
level that brings spells, a Builds tab with talent builds level by level (Naowh's, saved, imported,
edited, followed as you level), a mini bar, a level-up toast, a panel beside your class trainer, a
Trainable tab under the spellbook, a warlock's demon books, in the planner and at the demon trainer, and
Next Talent, a glow on the next talent of a build on the talent window. Laid out like the [Dungeon Journal](../NaowhForever_DungeonJournal/README.md), on
[`Shared/`](../Shared/README.md).

## Layout

```
NaowhForever_Training/
  NaowhForever_Training.toc   metadata, and Training.xml
  Training.xml       every file, in load order
  Training.lua       settings (ns.TrainingSettings), the module table (ns.Training) and what it keeps:
                     each character's prices and skipped spells, change listeners
  Constants.lua      the numbers several files share: max level, talent points, name length (Training.C)
  Data/
    TrainingData.lua   every spell each class learns as it levels, generated by Tools/build/training.py
    TrainingBuilds.lua each class's talent tree, generated by Tools/build/training_builds.py
    TrainingWeapons.lua each class's weapon skills and who teaches them, generated by Tools/build/training_weapons.py
    TrainingGrimoires.lua each warlock demon's books, generated by Tools/build/training_grimoires.py
  Plan.lua           your class's spells by what stands in the way, and their totals (Training.Plan)
  Upgrades.lua       what a rank adds over the one before (Training.Compare, Training.Upgrade)
  Coins.lua          money as the planner writes it (Training.Coins)
  Route.lua          the nearest class trainer and the waypoint route through your professions' trainers
  Builds.lua         talent builds: check, edit, share, learn, follow
  Trainer.lua        the prices a class trainer asks, and the module's events
  Grimoires.lua      Demon Books: the ranks each demon knows, read while it is out (Training.Grimoires)
  Weapons.lua        your weapon skills: their level, the weapons you carry, a weapon master's price
                     after your reputation discount (Training.WeaponSkills, Training.WeaponPrice)
  NextTalent.lua     Next Talent: the next points to take from a build, and each character's pick of
                     build for the talent window (Training.Next)
  View/
    Style.lua        the planner's own look, over Shared/Style.lua (Training.Style)
    Rows.lua         pooled cards, rows and section titles, a spell's tooltip and menu (Training.Rows)
    Spells.lua       the Spells tab: next visit, road to 60, spell lists (Training.Spells)
    Builds.lua       the Builds tab: builds, steps, the editor (Talents and Spells tabs) (Training.BuildsView)
  UI/
    Place.lua        windows on whole pixels, kept account-wide
    Window.lua       the window (/nftraining, ns.OpenTrainingWindow, ns.ToggleTrainingWindow)
    MiniBar.lua      the mini bar
    WeaponSkills.lua the Weapon Skills panel, on the shared tracker panel
    Toast.lua        the Level-Up Toast
    TrainerPanel.lua the Panel at the Trainer
    DemonTrainer.lua Demon Books' marks at the merchant and the line in a book's tooltip
    Spellbook.lua    the Trainable tab under the spellbook and its panel
    TalentWindows.lua the open talent window (Blizzard's or EllesmereUI's), and a talent's button
    TalentMark.lua   the glow on the next talent
    TalentDrawer.lua the side panel beside the talent window, and the button that opens it
    NextTalent.lua   turns Next Talent on and off, and redraws it while a talent window is open
    SettingsPage.lua its settings page (Training Planner/Settings), declared as cards
```

`Training.View` is the window's state (its frames, the tab, the level and build shown) that the
view files and the window share.

## Why

- Followed builds use GUIDs in `trainingFollow` to separate same-name characters. Legacy
  name-realm entries are copied only for a matching class and retained for other characters.
  `false` marks a stopped or empty choice so migration cannot restore an old selection.
- The on/off switches' defaults (`enabled`, `levelUpToast`, `trainerPanel`, `spellbook`, `demonBooks`) are read from
  `ns.FEATURES.training`; the Trainer Popup card's switch belongs to the QoL store, and QoL's `Questing/Trainer.lua` declares
  that card on this page, so it goes when QoL is off.
- TrainingData rows are `{ level, spellID, cost, flags }` in level order; cost is the trainer's base
  price in copper: what its window asked, or What's Training's list of it (MIT), for a class whose
  trainer we have read (`Tools/data/trainer_prices.json`), else Wowhead's, which predates Forever's
  launch prices, or 0 when Wowhead has none (a few new spells), which reads "At the trainer".
  Flags: `quest` (taught by a quest), `needs` (the rank to learn first), `talent`
  (the talent it needs), `races` (the races that learn it), `replacedBy` (the level 60 book's rank that
  replaces it). The trainer window updates a price to what it actually asked.
- Prices are kept per character, by GUID (`Shared.CharacterData`): a trainer's price carries that
  character's reputation discount. The account-wide prices kept before (`trainingPrices`) carried
  another character's discount, so they are cleared at login; they were only a copy of what trainers
  asked.
- Weapon skills are not in TrainingData: Wowhead lists them at level 0 with no price and no trainer, and
  the class trainer does not teach them. The weapon masters of the capitals do, so TrainingWeapons rows
  are `{ level, spellID, 0, masters = { names } }`, the names being the Weapon Master NPCs of
  `Shared/Game/Towns.lua`. The level is the game's own MinLevel for the class (Polearms, Dual Wield),
  else 1; Dual Wield has no master (the class trainer teaches it). The Weapon Skills section of the
  Spells tab lists the ones you lack, and a click puts a waypoint on the nearest master of your faction.
  No price is known, so they read "At the trainer".
- The Weapon Skills panel reads a skill's level from `C_SkillInfo.GetSkillLineInfoByID`, by the skill
  line each weapon spell belongs to (`SKILL_BY_SPELL`), and the weapons you carry by their item
  subclass (`SPELL_BY_SUBCLASS`). Known skills show only for the weapons you wear or carry unless
  Show All Known Skills is on; the ones you lack always show unless Show Unlearned Skills is off.
- A weapon master asks 10 silver (`BASE_COST`), less their city's reputation discount: 5% Friendly,
  10% Honored, 15% Revered, 20% Exalted. `MASTER_FACTION` names each master's city; Bixi Wobblebonk
  is Gnomeregan's, and Hanashi and Sayoc are Orgrimmar's. The price is the nearest master's, the one a
  click puts a waypoint on.
- Wands are not in TrainingWeapons (casters start with them), so the panel adds Wands only once it is
  known.
- The panel saves its top-left corner and is placed once, when it is built: the shared tracker panel
  saves the anchor the game gives a dragged frame, and re-placing it on every reapply moved it as its
  height changed.
- A talent node is `{ spell, ranks, row, column, slot in the row, the node it needs at full rank }`; a
  build's points are nodes, one per point from level 10, 51 in all, 5 in a column for each row above.
- Blizzard reuses a node for a new talent when it reworks a tree (the 30 October 2026 hotfix put Primal Bite
  in Berserk's node), so a node in an old build can name another talent now. Each class tree has a revision
  (`rev`) and `replaced`, the revision at which each node came in or took another talent; a saved build
  carries the revision it was made on. The first time a build made on an older tree is read, its points in
  replaced nodes go, then every other point that no longer fits the tree, keeping the rest in order, so
  following it never buys a talent the player did not pick. An imported string carries no revision and is
  read against today's tree.
- A talent's spell shows its full rank's text, so its tooltip reads the text of its rank and the next from
  the node's trait entry (GetTraitEntry), as the game's talent frame does. Another class's nodes are not in
  your talent config, so their tooltip shows the spell.
- A rank counts as learned once a later rank is: some ranks replace the one before. Dire Bear Form
  replaces Bear Form in the spellbook the same way, so it needs Bear Form (the game's SupercedesSpell),
  and the planner names Bear Form where it would name the rank before. A level 60 book's
  rank replaces a trainer's rank too, but no trainer sells it, so it is not in the data and the rank it
  replaces names it (`replacedBy`). A rank before that no trainer sells (granted at level 1) is not in
  the data, so the game is asked. PLAYER_LEVEL_UP says the new level before UnitLevel does, so the
  toast plans for the level it was given.
- Forever reports "Unknown" for the player's name early in loading. Until the name is known, the
  character's skipped spells and followed build are kept in a table that is never saved: an
  `Unknown-<realm>` entry in the account store would be shared by every character.
- The trainer's list has names and required levels but no spell IDs, so a service is the class spell
  of that name and level; a header has no level and matches nothing. A pet trainer shares the class
  trainer's window but none of its services is a class spell. Forever's service kind can be missing
  (seen at profession trainers); then it is offered once its level is reached.
- Learning goes from the last index first: buying a service can renumber the ones after it, and one
  whose index now names another service is skipped.
- What a rank adds is the first number in its description that grew and is not a time or a distance,
  named by up to two words after it (or before it); two descriptions that do not line up show none.
  Descriptions the client has not loaded are asked for, and arrive as SPELL_DATA_LOAD_RESULT; a burst
  of them draws once, 0.1s later.
- The nearest trainer is your class's for your faction on your continent; with none there, or no
  position (in an instance), one in your faction's capital, then a shared one.
- An imported build is data, never run: its class must have a tree here, its points must pass the
  rules, and its name loses escape codes and line breaks and is cut to 40 letters.
- The Import box takes at most 4,000 letters (`PASTE_MAX`): pasting a long string, such as a profile,
  into an edit box hangs the game. The longest build code the addon can write is under 1,000.
- A build is kept by a number of its own, given the first time it is asked for, or a built-in one by
  its place in the data: names repeat, so they cannot be keys. Committing talents fires the talent
  events that start a followed build's learning again, so learning is guarded while it runs.
- A saved build can carry spells to skip (`skip`, spell IDs by rank, set in the editor, any spell of the
  class whether learned or not). The build you follow decides what Learn Selected at the trainer leaves out
  and what the Spells tab calls skipped, next to the character's own right-click skips. Those own skips
  are kept per character, so they never reach a new one; the build's are account-wide, travel in a
  build's export string and the Profiles share, and a new character gets them by following the build.
- The editor's Spells tab lists a spell with more than one rank on a row of its own, which skips every
  rank, then each rank on its own row. A skip has always been kept by rank, the whole-spell row writing
  every rank's ID, so builds saved or shared before skips by rank still skip every rank.
- Coins are written as text, each coin's letter in its colour: the game's coin icons blur when drawn
  large.
- The window and the mini bar sit on whole screen pixels, and are sized in whole pixels, or every
  line and letter in them blurs. Its dots are drawn from the circle art itself, tinted, without pixel
  snapping, which would leave small circles jagged.
- Text has a soft drop shadow, which reads sharper than flat text on the dark cards.
- Forever's spellbook is `PlayerSpellsFrame.SpellBookFrame` in the load-on-demand `Blizzard_PlayerSpells`, so the
  Spellbook Tab hooks it once that addon loads, and only after the switch is first turned on. It only post-hooks
  the book's OnShow and OnHide: its tab and panel are parented to UIParent and anchored to the book, never put
  inside it, and are drawn a frame later from a timer, outside the game's own show. Nothing of the book's is
  called, changed or hidden: it casts from its own buttons' clicks, which must stay untainted.
- The book has no secure frames on Forever, so the tab and panel show in combat too.
- Under Forever's Gamepad interface style (`ns.GamepadStandDown()`) the Spellbook Tab and the Panel at
  the Trainer stay off. That style opens and navigates the spellbook and the trainer from secure
  code, and our OnShow hooks on them would run inside it and taint it.
- The tab sits where the game anchors its own bottom tabs (`TAB_X`, `TAB_Y`: 22 in, 2 up); Forever hides those,
  as its book and talents open as separate windows.
- A demon book is an item whose use spell teaches the pet (SpellEffect 36 aimed at the pet, target 5) a spell
  on a demon's own skill line (its CreatureFamily's first SkillLine), and that a warlock may use. That is 59
  books: the Imp's 17, the Voidwalker's 21, the Succubus's 11 and the Felhunter's 10; the Felguard has none.
  The other 97 "Grimoire of ..." items in the game's tables teach the warlock, or a spell no skill line has.
- A book's level and price are its item's RequiredLevel and BuyPrice, the same in Classic Era's tables. Which
  books a demon trainer stocks, and at what price, the server decides.
- A book row is `{ level, pet spell, price, item = , needs = }`, shaped like a TrainingData row, so its row,
  price and what its rank adds come from the same code. `needs` is the rank it replaces (SupercedesSpell).
- A pet's spells can be read only while it is out (`C_SpellBook.IsSpellKnown` on the Pet bank), so the ranks
  each demon knows are kept per character (`trainingDemonBooks`, by GUID, then by the demon's skill line) and
  read again on UNIT_PET, SPELLS_CHANGED and PET_BAR_UPDATE. A demon never summoned says to summon it once.
- The demon out is told by UnitCreatureFamily's ID, so any client language works. That ID is secret while
  unit identity is restricted, and nothing is read then.
- A pet's spellbook is read only once it has one of that demon's spells (a book's spell or the rank before
  it; rank 1 comes with the summon). Just after a summon it can still be empty or the last demon's, and a
  read then would mark every book unlearned.
- A book counts as learned once its rank or a later one is known, as the planner's spells do.
- The Incubus is on the Succubus's skill line (205), so they learn from the same books and share a section.
- The API has no "already known" for a pet: an item tooltip's line types (Enum.TooltipDataLineType) have no
  such line, and MerchantItemInfo's isUsable is about class and level. So the marks come from what the demon
  was last seen to know.
- Forever's merchant is the Mainline one: every refresh of its item buttons goes through
  `MerchantFrame_Update`, which a hooksecurefunc follows from the first time Demon Books is on. The marks are
  `Parts.ItemBadge` corners on the merchant's own item buttons: a tick (`common-icon-checkmark`, from
  Forever's character creation) when learned, the green upgrade arrow when it can learn it now, the clock
  when it is not your level yet, none for a demon never summoned. The book's tooltip line says which.
- Under the Gamepad interface style the marks stay off, as the Panel at the Trainer does: that style
  navigates the merchant from secure code. The tooltip line stays.
- The books are not in the road's totals or the next visit: they come from another trainer, and a book for a
  demon never summoned may be known already.
- Next Talent is off by default, and nothing of it is made while it is off: no frame, event or hook until it
  is first turned on. Turning it off again unregisters its events and hides what it drew.
- While it is on, the talent events are registered only while a talent window is open, and
  SPELL_DATA_LOAD_RESULT only while a talent's name is still loading. A burst of events draws once, a frame
  later. The model tables and their rows are reused, so a redraw makes no new tables.
- A talent's rank is the larger of its `currentRank` and `activeRank`: `currentRank` changes as you click a
  point in, before you apply, so the glow moves with your clicks.
- Picking a build in the panel only lights it. It does not follow it, so nothing is bought for you. The pick
  is kept per character, by GUID (`trainingNextPick`), with the class it was made on. With no pick, the panel
  lights the build you follow and says that following spends its points for you.
- A talent's button is found with the game's own lookups (GetTalentButtonByNodeID, then
  EnumerateAllTalentButtons). EllesmereUI's Classic Talents has neither, so its shown children are searched,
  at most 6 deep. A button not drawn yet is looked for again on the next frames, at most 10 times
  (`MAX_RETRIES`).
- The glow is a frame of its own on the talent's button, in the button's own border shape, tinted with the
  theme's accent. Nothing of the button's is changed or hidden. The windows' OnShow and OnHide are
  post-hooked (HookScript), and EllesmereUI's window, made the first time it opens, is found through a
  hooksecurefunc on `EllesmereUI.RegisterEscapeClose`.
- The panel is a `Parts.SidePanel` on UIParent beside the window, so its build menu draws over the talent
  tree and not under it.
- The panel's button copies the Town Pins button on the world map: 32 square, the logo 4 in, on a 0.9 dark
  fill, so it stands out on the talent window. Its border stays lit while the panel is open. Like the Town
  Pins button in the map's corner, it sits in the corner of the talent trees, under the Unspent Talents
  count: 64 below the search box's bottom right corner (`UNDER_SEARCH`).
- In the panel, the next talent's name and its cost sit `BIG_TEXT_DROP` (1px) inside the top and bottom of
  its icon, where the Naowh font's letters line up with the icon's edges.
- Under the Gamepad interface style Next Talent stays off, as the Spellbook Tab does: that style moves
  through the game's windows from secure code, and hooks on them would taint it.
