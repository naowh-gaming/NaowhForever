# Discovery

The library books and the Cozy Sleeping Bag: where to find each one, on the map and nearby, and
every quest and rare by zone (the Quest List and Rares tabs, in `QuestList/`, see its README). Its
Library Books tab has every book for your faction by zone, where it lies, whether you carry it,
and your road to the Friend of the Library rewards; its Sleeping Bag tab the bag's hidden quest
chain, step by step. Each has a tracker and map pins, and the books a nearby alert and a waypoint
to the nearest one. Its Attunements
tab has the quest chain for each dungeon key and attunement, the step you are on first, with a
tracker and map pins of its own, and a Class page with your class's own quest chains, Tier 0.5's
(Dungeon Set 2) first. Its Field
Journal tab is a page each for fish, herbs and dishes: every one there is, where to find it, and
how many this character has caught, picked or cooked. Off by default, every feature too: players
turn it on in its settings pages (Discovery/Library Books, Discovery/Sleeping Bag,
Discovery/Attunements and Discovery/Field Journal).

## Layout

```
NaowhForever_Discovery/
  NaowhForever_Discovery.toc  its metadata, and one file line: Discovery.xml
  Discovery.xml               every file, in load order
  Discovery.lua               its settings (ns.DiscoverySettings) and the module table (ns.Discovery)
  Constants.lua               the numbers several files share
  Data/
    Books.lua                 the books, where each lies and who takes it, and the reward quests
    SleepingBag.lua           the Cozy Sleeping Bag's steps, for each faction
    Attunements.lua           the dungeon keys and attunements, each with the quest that gives it, and
                              the Tier 0.5 questline for each class and faction
    FieldJournal.lua          every fish, herb and dish the Field Journal lists, generated by
                              Tools/build/field_journal.py
  Library.lua                 the book rules: yours, carried, handed in, rewards, waypoints (ns.Library)
  SleepingBag.lua             the chain's rules: your steps, the one to do now (ns.SleepingBagChain)
  Attunements.lua             the attunement rules: each key's quests in order, the one you are on,
                              your class's own chains (Discovery.Attune)
  Field.lua                   the Field Journal's rules: what this character caught, picked and cooked,
                              ranks and where-lines (Discovery.Field)
  Nearby.lua                  the nearby alert: a ping and a chat line near a book you need, and
                              the Nearest Book waypoint
  View/
    Style.lua                 Discovery's own look, on top of Shared/Style.lua
    View.lua                  the window's row kinds, and the row a book, a step and a journal entry share
    BooksHero.lua             the Library Books card: the road to each reward
    BookRow.lua               a book
    BagHero.lua               the Sleeping Bag card
    StepRow.lua               a step of the chain
    AttuneRow.lua             a quest of an attunement
    FieldHero.lua             the Field Journal card: a page's count and bar
    FieldRow.lua              a fish, herb or dish
  UI/
    Window.lua                the window (/nfdiscovery) and its six tabs; Quest List and Rares are drawn by QuestList/UI/Window.lua
    BookTracker.lua           the Library Books tracker
    BagTracker.lua            the Sleeping Bag tracker
    AttuneTracker.lua         the Attunements tracker
    BookPins.lua, .xml        the book pins on the world map, and their template
    BagPins.lua, .xml         the chain's step pins on the world map, and their template
    AttunePins.lua, .xml      each attunement's next quest on the world map, and its template
    BooksSettings.lua         the Discovery/Library Books settings page, declared as cards
    BagSettings.lua           the Discovery/Sleeping Bag settings page, declared as cards
    AttuneSettings.lua        the Discovery/Attunements settings page, declared as cards
    FieldSettings.lua         the Discovery/Field Journal settings page, declared as cards
  QuestList/                  the Quest List and Rares (Completo until 2026-10-10): its own README
```

Each layer only uses the ones above it. Everything the module shares hangs off `ns.Discovery`;
`ns.Library`, `ns.SleepingBagChain`, `ns.LibraryBooks`, `ns.LibraryGoals`, `ns.LibraryTurnIns`,
`ns.SleepingBag`, `ns.DiscoverySettings`, `ns.OpenDiscoveryWindow` and `ns.ToggleDiscoveryWindow`
keep their names for the rest of the addon.

## The data

- `ns.LibraryBooks`: the 40 library books on WoW Forever. On load, each book's item id is
  copied onto `ns.LibraryBookItems` (Shared) so another module can exclude those items without
  reading this list. Positions from
  foreverchanges.pro/library-books (2026-09-24, build 1.60.1.70009), cross-checked with
  zockify.com and Wowhead Forever; the turn-in quest IDs from Wowhead Forever's quests. Ataeric's
  spot and Magma or Lava?'s are their objects' spawns on Wowhead Forever (410299, 463206), and the
  sides of Theocritus, Rumi and the Apothecary's Primer its quests' (both sides, both librarians take
  them), 2026-11-07, and Ta'zo's likewise, 2026-11-24. Each book:
  `quest` (its turn-in, flagged completed once handed in), `item` (the book), `tier` (its set's
  quest level), `side` ("A", "H" or "B" for both), `turnIn` ("librarian", or "trainer": the level
  60 set goes to a mage trainer), and `spots`, each `{ uiMapID, x, y, place, note }`; a book with
  two spots lies in either. `unplaced`: nobody has found it on Forever yet; `missing`: it cannot be
  had on Forever. Both have no spots and are left off the map, and a missing book off every count.
- The Eastern Plaguelands spots, and the Stormwind librarian and mage trainer, are on Forever's
  redrawn maps (the trainer and librarian from Wowhead Forever's NPC spots; Classic's were about 200
  yards off on Forever's Stormwind). The Blackrock
  Mountain books are pinned on the mountain in Burning Steppes, since the mountain's own map is not
  the zone you stand in.
- `ns.LibraryGoals`: handing in 10, 20 and 25 books each earns a reward quest; the books count
  toward every one. `rewards` are the choice of items, `{ itemID, name }`, the name for before the
  client has the item; `level` the level the quest needs. `reported`: the book count is what
  players report (method.gg, 2026-10-04); Wowhead Forever has the quest (82208, its rewards, level 35
  since build 1.60.1.70170) but not how many books it takes. The 20-book quest needs level 20.
- `ns.SleepingBag`: a hidden quest chain across Azeroth, each step a thing in the world to click,
  ending in the Cozy Sleeping Bag. From Wowhead Forever's quests (objects and spots) and its guide
  (the climbs and jumps), 2026-10-04. `steps[side]` in order: `object` to click, `map`, `x`, `y`,
  `place`, `tip` (how to get there where it is not plain), and `done`, the quest clicking it hands
  in; the first has `started`, the quest it starts, instead. The Alliance goes from Westfall to The
  Barrens and the Horde the other way ("... and that note you found"); from Stonetalon on both share
  the same steps. `level` is 14, what the chain needs; `optional` a side step not needed for the bag.
- `ns.Attunements`: `list`, the dungeon keys and attunements a quest gives, in level order: `dungeon`,
  `item` (the key) and `quest`, the quest that gives it (a quest ID, or `{ A = ID, H = ID }` per
  faction). The same keys and quests as the Dungeon Journal's `Data/Keys.lua`, kept here too because
  Discovery runs without the Journal; the Seal of Ascension names 4743, the quest that gives it, which
  the Quest List has (Wowhead Forever's quest rewards the seal). Molten Core's is Attunement to the
  Core (7848), a quest and no key, so it has no `item`, and `icon`, the Core Fragment it asks for,
  for its map pin. The steps are not stored: they are the quest's prerequisites in the Quest List's
  data (`D.Requires`), walked back to the first. `inside`: for a step with no spot in that data, by
  quest ID, `{ dungeon, giver }`: the dungeon it is given in and who gives it, from Wowhead Forever's
  quest pages (2026-11-14): each page's category (Blackrock Depths, Blackrock Spire, Maraudon) and its
  Start. A Crumpled Up Note has no giver, a note that drops in the dungeon starts it. The Journal's
  quest list puts Seal of Ascension in the lower spire. Tier 0.5's steps given inside Stratholme, Dire
  Maul and Upper Blackrock Spire are from the same pages (2026-11-25).
- Tier 0.5 (`class = true`, on the Class page) is Dungeon Set 2's questline, An Earnest Proposition to
  Saving the Best for Last, 29 steps. Its `steps` are kept by hand, from Wowhead Forever's quest pages
  (2026-11-25): a quest ID, `{ A = ID, H = ID }` per faction, a list of IDs of which Bodley gives you
  one, or the name of a class step (`proposition`, `compensation`, `parting`, `best`). `classQuests`
  gives each class step for each faction and class as `{ shown, forever }`: `shown` is the Quest List's
  quest the row shows (its name, giver and spot), `forever` the quest Forever gives now. Forever
  replaced the classic class quests (8905 to 9022) in build 1.60.1.70862 to 71037 with its own
  (84147 to 84210, from a new Deliana and Mokvar), and added 97411 to 97414 for the Horde paladin and
  97447 to 97450 for the Alliance shaman, who had none; the class of each comes from its reward, the
  class's own Dungeon Set 2 piece. Those quests are not in the Quest List's data (its quest list does
  not carry them), so a step shows the classic quest of the same name: the class's own, or for the
  Alliance shaman and the Horde paladin another class's of their faction. `notes`: a line for a step's
  tooltip, by quest ID: Forever's Three Kings of Flame asks for the Ash of Asharell (279415), which
  Executor Asharell (270451) drops in the Burning Steppes, not the Cinder of Cynders.
- `ns.FieldJournal`: the Field Journal's fish, herbs and dishes, from `Tools/build/field_journal.py`
  (its docstring has the sources). `fish`: `item`, `skill` (the lowest Fishing skill that fishes in
  one of the zones it names), `zones` (its three best, `uiMapID, chance` pairs, the chance a cast there
  catches it, in percent) and `schools` (fishing pools give it too). A fish with no zones and no skill
  comes only from pools, and the database has too few pool spawns to say where they are. `herbs`:
  `item`, `skill`, `zones` (the three with the most spots) and `nodes` (the herb nodes that yield it,
  by object ID). `dishes`: `spell`, `item`, `skill`, `how` ("trainer", "vendor", "quest", "drop",
  "world" or "unknown") and who teaches it for each side, `A`, `H` or `B` for both: a vendor
  `{ name, uiMapID, x, y }`, a quest `{ quest, uiMapID, x, y, giver }`, a boss `{ name }`.

## Why

- The window has two scroll frames on one spot, one for Library Books and Sleeping Bag and one for the
  Quest List and Rares. A scroll bar is a child of the window, not of its scroll, so `UI.SlimScroll` hides
  and shows it with the scroll; left alone, the hidden page's bar stayed up over the shown page's and took
  its clicks.
- Book and Sleeping Bag pins scale with `ns.Shared.ScalePin` at `BOOK_PIN_SCALE` / `BAG_PIN_SCALE` (0.5),
  like the quest pins: drawn in map units they grew with the canvas and filled the full screen map. Pin
  Size 20 is the default every map pin shares.
- Every file registers nothing but a login check until its feature is switched on.
- The windows' opacity was one setting (`windowAlpha`) before each had its own: a player who set
  it keeps it on the trackers until they set theirs, once.
- The bank count is the client's copy from the last time the bank was open.
- Caves and buildings have maps of their own under the zone, and the books are placed on the
  zone's, so the zone you stand in walks up to it.
- The reward quests count the books handed to the librarian, so progress counts those only.
- A step is done once the quest it hands in is; the first once the quest it starts is in your log
  or handed in. Two steps of one name (the Burned-Out Remains) are told apart by their zone.
- An attunement's steps are the quests its last quest needs, each after the ones it needs, for your
  faction; of several quests of which one will do, the first that is yours. You are on the step after
  the last one handed in, so a quest the game never flagged before it does not hold the chain back.
  The tab reads the Quest List's data, which loads the first time it is opened, or at login with
  its tracker or pins on.
- Tier 0.5's steps are written down rather than walked: the Quest List's data has only the classic
  class quests, so the walk would show quests Forever no longer gives, and none at all for a Horde
  paladin or an Alliance shaman. A step is done once its shown quest or Forever's own is handed in, so a
  classic quest done before Forever replaced it still counts. A step of Bodley's that is one of four is
  done once any of them is; its next quest is the one in your log, or else the first.
- The Class page lists Tier 0.5 first, then the Quest List's chains that start with a quest only your
  class can take, by level, then your class's other quests that are in no such chain. A class quest in a chain that
  starts with an ordinary quest is listed among the others: the chain is not a class chain. Repeatable
  quests are left out, a holiday's quests while it is off, and a quest another one closed (Heeding the
  Call from the other trainers). The Quest List's own An Earnest Proposition chain is left out: Tier 0.5
  shows it. A step of several quests is a row for each; one handed in shows done
  while the rest of its step is still to do, and one the game does not offer yet waits as Later.
- A key is one to work on (`Attune.Next`) until its last quest is handed in or you carry it, once you
  have started it or are of its first quest's level. The Attunements tracker has a row for each, with
  its next quest; it hides when there is none, and its X closes it until you enter another zone, as
  the other trackers' does. The map has one pin for each, at who gives its next quest.
- The tracker does nothing before login: which quests are yours is kept by the character's name, which
  the game does not have until then.
- The hint lines keep the light blue they always had, unless the theme changed the Accent; then
  they take the theme's lighter Accent (`SoftBlue`).
- Map pins override `CheckMouseButtonPassthrough` with nothing: the map calls it on every pin, and
  its `SetPassThroughButtons` is protected, so calling it from our refresh is blocked in combat.
  Our pins want their clicks. The pin mixins are globals so the XML templates can name them.
- A pin's icon is cropped 0.08 to 0.92 to cut off the icon's own border.
- The Library Books tracker pops up on entering a zone with books you still need and stays while
  you are in that zone, even once they are looted. Its X closes it until you enter another zone,
  and switches Always Show off. With Always Show it is up in every zone with a dropdown; entering a
  zone with books selects it, and a zone picked after that sticks until you enter another. The
  picked zone stays in the list at 0 once its last book is looted, so the pick does not jump.
- Looting and handing in fire several events at once; the trackers redraw once, 0.2 seconds later,
  when the quest log has the change too.
- Unlock Mode shows the tracker on your zone, or the first zone with books for your faction (The
  Barrens for the Horde, Westfall for the Alliance).
- The Sleeping Bag tracker shows from level 14 until you have the bag; its X closes it until you
  enter another zone, as the Library Books tracker's does.
- Two steps at one spot (the Messenger Bag and the satchel under it) share a pin: the first not
  done. Its pins redraw once, on the quest log update after a step is taken or handed in.
- The game says when you start and stop moving but not where you are, so the nearby alert checks
  your distance once a second while you move, and once more as you stop; standing still nothing
  runs, and nothing at all in a zone with no book left. `REARM` is 1.5: past one and a half times
  the range the alert rearms, so standing at its edge does not repeat it.
- Nearest Book is a guide waypoint (`ns.SetGuideWaypoint`) on the closest book in your zone. It
  moves only once that book is looted or handed in, not as you walk, so it never jumps between two
  books at a similar distance. It is placed only when no waypoint is up or its own still is, and
  cleared only through its own `details`; dismissed on the Navigator Bar it stays away until the
  next zone. It needs no movement events: zone changes, loot and hand-ins redraw it.
- On the road a stripe stands for each book, in the accent for those handed in, with YOU over the
  end of the last one. A reward's dot is filled grey once handed in, in the accent once ready (or
  waiting on your level), a ring while ahead; once handed in, the reward you have keeps its colour
  and the others grey.
- A waypoint from the window or the tracker also opens the map with Open the Map (out of combat
  only); a pin's waypoint does not.
- The Field Journal counts for each character, in `discoveryField` by the character's GUID: a fish or
  herb by its item, a dish by its recipe's spell. A count of 0 is one ticked off by hand, from before
  the journal was on, and Shift-click again clears it. Shift-click never clears a real count.
- A catch counts when its loot slot is taken (`LOOT_SLOT_CLEARED`), not when the loot window opens, so
  a fish left in the water counts nothing. A fish counts only from fishing loot (`IsFishingLoot`), a
  herb only from one of the herb nodes in `nodes`, so a herb from a creature, a chest or Stranglekelp
  fished up is no pick.
- A dish counts on `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED` with its recipe's spell, heard only while a profession
  window is open (`TRADE_SKILL_SHOW` to `TRADE_SKILL_CLOSE`): cooking needs the window, and casts in
  combat cost nothing.
- Loot links, loot sources, quantities and the cast's spell are checked with `issecretvalue` first.
- A page's sections are the profession ranks its skills need: Apprentice to 75, Journeyman to 150,
  Expert to 225, Artisan above (Forever's Plaguelands waters ask for 330). Fish only pools give have
  no skill and come last, under Schools of fish.
- The Fish, Herbs and Cooking switch sits where the Quest List's search box does, which only the
  Quest List and Rares tabs show. The tab bar is capped to leave it `SEARCH_MIN_W`: in longer languages
  the six tabs' labels shorten instead of running under it.
- With the journal off the tab still lists everything and where to find it; only the counting waits
  for its switch. Its lookups by item and spell are made the first time they are needed.
- A journal row's skill column is `LEVEL_EXTRA` (8) wider than a book's level: skills run to three
  digits, which the book column would cut off.
